### FULL (About page / expanded bio)

**ABOUT THE AUTHOR**

Bruno writes about pain from the inside — every kind of it.

A former professional footballer, he has carried physical pain since the age of seventeen: multiple knee operations, and the arthritis they left behind, mean he has never known the subject only from a textbook — he has known it from the treatment table, the recovery room, and every ordinary day since. But he has also known the pains that don't show on any scan — losing everything, being broken, being deceived, and the long, unglamorous work of getting back up, being restored, and finding healing for his own emotions. He isn't writing from the safe side of the river; he is writing from the far bank, having crossed — which is exactly why he can tell others the way.

That long road is what led him into rehabilitation. For decades he has studied — and still studies — across the many fields that touch pain, from sports and clinical therapy to a wide range of hands-on and modern treatments, driven by one stubborn question: how do you actually help a person heal, and not simply manage a symptom?

Yet pain, for him, was never only physical. For more than seventeen years, alongside his clinical work, he has walked with people through the harder, quieter kinds of suffering — counselling individuals and couples, through marriages and through personal crises, in different countries and cultures, with people of deep faith and of none at all. It taught him what the science would later confirm: that the body, the mind and the spirit are never really separate.

Today he brings those worlds together — a sports and clinical therapist's practice in Ipswich, and work spanning health, technology and business, including a programme in artificial intelligence at the University of Oxford (2021) and a background in film and marketing that shapes how he tells a story. Different disciplines, one thread: taking the whole person seriously.

*Beyond Pain* is the book he wishes he'd had — for the people he treats, and for the younger man who was once on the surgeon's table.

*"My purpose is simple: to treat every person the way I wish I'd been treated during my own recovery — with real attention, not just protocol."*
