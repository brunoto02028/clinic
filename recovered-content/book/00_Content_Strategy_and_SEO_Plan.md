# BPR Content Strategy & SEO Plan
### Prioritised, evidence-based article roadmap for a sports & clinical rehab clinic in Ipswich

## The short version
Write "hub" articles for the highest-demand, high-disability conditions BPR does not yet cover — **knee osteoarthritis, neck pain / "tech neck", shoulder (rotator cuff) pain, and Achilles tendinopathy** — then build topic clusters around BPR's existing back/knee/foot content. These fill genuine gaps, match huge UK demand, and are squarely in a rehab clinic's wheelhouse.

## Why (key findings)
- **~1 in 3 people in the UK live with a musculoskeletal (MSK) condition**; MSK is the biggest global contributor to years lived with disability. Most prevalent: **back pain, osteoarthritis, neck pain** (Versus Arthritis, *State of Musculoskeletal Health*).
- **Work and screens are shifting the burden to the neck, shoulder and upper limb.** Upper limb/neck now account for ~41% of work-related MSDs (HSE 2024/25); office workers have the highest incidence of neck disorders; up to ~21% of remote workers report daily neck/back/shoulder pain.
- **What people search matches this:** the most-Googled pain topics are headache, low back, neck, knee and shoulder pain. Knee pain is the 4th most-searched UK symptom (~13,490/month); people search symptom-first ("why does my knee hurt", "X exercises") then higher-intent ("physio near me").
- **Running drives a predictable set of injuries:** patellofemoral pain (~17%), shin splints, plantar fasciitis, ITB syndrome and Achilles tendinopathy (~10%). Strong local audience via the **Ipswich Half Marathon** and **Suffolk parkruns**.
- **Local competitors are service-page heavy and content-light** — an opening for clear, myth-busting, guideline-referenced patient education plus local intent ("physio Ipswich", condition + Suffolk).

2. **Kneecap Pain (Patellofemoral Pain) / "Runner's Knee"** — most common running injury; image on file. *Refs: Willy et al. 2019 JOSPT CPG; Collins et al. 2018 BJSM consensus.*

## Cluster structure
- **Spine & nerve** (pillar: back pain) → sciatica, chronic LBP, cervical radiculopathy.
- **Neck & desk-worker** (pillar: Tech Neck) → headaches, tennis elbow, carpal tunnel, desk setup.
- **Knee** (pillar: Knee OA) → patellofemoral, cartilage, meniscus, patellar tendinopathy, ITB, OA myths.
- **Hip** (pillar: GTPS) → hip OA, snapping hip, proximal hamstring tendinopathy.
- **Shoulder** (pillar: Rotator cuff) → frozen shoulder.
- **Foot/ankle & running** (pillar: Return to Running) → plantar fasciitis, Achilles, shin splints, ankle, patellofemoral.
- **Recovery & performance** (existing): HRV, sleep, photobiomodulation, prevention, shockwave — cross-link into every cluster.

## Local SEO (Ipswich / Suffolk)
- Target "physio Ipswich", "sports therapy Suffolk", "[condition] Ipswich".
- Tie running content to the Ipswich Half Marathon (September) and Christchurch Park / Suffolk parkruns.
- Add a "where to get physio for X in Ipswich" FAQ block on each pillar; align with Google Business Profile.

## Safety / red-flag sections required
Low back pain (cauda equina), neck/cervical radiculopathy (myelopathy, trauma), shoulder (acute cuff tear, hot joint), headache (thunderclap, new >50, neuro signs), carpal tunnel (thenar wasting), knee/hip OA (hot swollen joint → rule out inflammatory/septic).

## Reference discipline
Cite NICE (NG226, NG59, CKS), JOSPT/BJSM CPGs, Cochrane reviews and named landmark RCTs in Harvard style. Verify every reference (e.g. LEAP trial is *BMJ* 2018;361:k1662). Run live keyword checks before publishing to confirm titles and capture "People Also Ask" questions as subheadings.
