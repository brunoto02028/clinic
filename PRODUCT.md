# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Primary: Physiotherapists and clinic administrators (UK-based) managing patient records, appointments, clinical notes, foot scans, and body assessments. They work in a clinical environment, often between patient sessions, and need quick access to information.

Secondary: Patients accessing their health portal to view appointments, clinical notes, exercise plans, and scan results. Range from young athletes to elderly patients. Must be immediately understandable.

## Product Purpose

Bruno Physical Rehabilitation clinic management system. Handles the full patient lifecycle: intake/screening, appointment scheduling, clinical documentation (SOAP notes), biomechanical assessments (foot scans, body posture), treatment plans, exercise prescriptions, and patient education. Dual-panel system: admin (clinician-facing) and patient portal.

## Brand Personality

Clinical, trustworthy, modern. The interface should feel like a premium medical tool, not a generic SaaS dashboard. Confidence without coldness. Professional without being sterile.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards with white backgrounds and blue accents
- Admin templates (AdminLTE, CoreUI, generic Bootstrap dashboards)
- Overly dark "hacker" aesthetics with neon green
- Glassmorphism-heavy interfaces
- Gradient text, decorative blur cards, hero-metric templates
- Anything that looks like it was made by AI without design direction

## Design Principles

1. **Clinical clarity**: Information hierarchy serves clinical decision-making. The most important data is the most visible.
2. **One-click access**: Any primary function reachable in one click from the sidebar. No hunting through menus.
3. **Calm confidence**: The interface should feel authoritative and calm, like a well-run clinic. No visual noise, no unnecessary decoration.
4. **Bilingual native**: EN-GB and PT-BR are equal citizens. Layout must accommodate both without breaking.
5. **Progressive disclosure**: Show essentials first, details on demand. Empty states guide rather than frustrate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum
- Sufficient contrast for clinical environments (bright overhead lighting)
- Keyboard navigable for all primary actions
- Screen reader support for patient-facing portal
- Reduced motion alternatives for all animations
- Color-blind safe status indicators (don't rely on color alone)
